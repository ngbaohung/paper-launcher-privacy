Paper Launcher Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Paper Launcher ("Paper", "the app") is an Android home screen. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: Paper has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of its own. Your settings stay on your phone.

Information Paper does not collect

Paper does not collect your name, email address, contacts, messages, photos, browsing history or any other personal information, and it does not track you across apps or websites. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

Location (weather widget)

If you add the weather widget and grant location permission, Paper reads your device's location to show local weather.

  • Your coordinates are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, only to fetch the forecast. No account, name or device identifier is sent with them. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy is at open-meteo.com/en/terms.
  • To show the name of your city, Paper uses Android's built-in geocoder, which may be provided by Google on your device.
  • Paper keeps only the latest forecast on your phone. It does not keep a history of your locations.
  • Location is optional. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings; the weather widget then stops updating.

Music widget and notification access

Android requires a launcher to hold "notification access" before it can show and control the music that is currently playing. Paper uses this access only to read the active media player (song title, artist, artwork and playback controls). Paper never reads, stores or sends the contents of your notifications. You can turn this access off in Android Settings at any time.

Picture widget

Photos you choose for the picture widget are processed entirely on your phone, including the depth effect, which uses Google's on-device ML Kit. Your photos are not uploaded by Paper. Google Play services may download the ML Kit model and may collect limited, anonymous diagnostic data as described in Google's ML Kit terms.

Installed apps

As a launcher, Paper reads the list of apps installed on your phone so it can show and open them. This list stays on your phone.

Tips (in-app purchase)

Paper offers an optional tip through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; Paper never sees your card or payment details. Google's handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Settings backup

If you use the backup feature, Paper saves your settings to a file in a location you choose, and reads it back when you restore. The file is not sent anywhere by Paper.

Wallpaper

Paper can set its own paper texture as your wallpaper, but only after asking you. It does not read or save your existing wallpaper.

Data retention and deletion

Everything Paper stores (settings, the latest forecast, your widget layout) lives on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it.

Children

Paper is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
